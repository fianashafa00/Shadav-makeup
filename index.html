Hi, I'm Shadav

I'm a Makeup Artist who began my professional journey in 2022, inspired by a simple love for makeup and the special feeling it can bring. It all started with how beautiful, confident, and happy makeup made me feel, and I dreamed of creating that same feeling for others. Through Shadav Makeup, I want to offer more than a polished look: an experience that makes every woman feel beautiful, confident, and comfortable being herself.

Since 2022, I've grown my skills through graduation, special occasion, and bridal makeup. I also share my knowledge through private self-makeup classes and group makeup workshops. To me, being a makeup artist means always having room to learn, grow, and create something better.

“I believe beauty is not about becoming someone else. It's about discovering and embracing what already exists within you.”
