Terms & Conditions

Please read before booking. By paying your invoice, you agree to these terms.

Payment
  • A down payment (DP) of 50% of the total is required to secure your date. There is no booking without a DP.
  • Full payment is due no later than one day before the event.
  • Brides booking a makeup and attire package must also pay the attire deposit as specified.
  • Valid payments are made only to our BCA and Mandiri accounts under the name Fiana Davilia Shafa. Payments made to any other account are not our responsibility.
Rescheduling & Punctuality
  • Rescheduling is allowed once only, subject to the artist's availability. If the artist's schedule is full on the new date, the DP and full payment are forfeited.
  • Please be ready on time. If a client is an hour late and it may delay other appointments, we reserve the right to leave the location and cancel the agreement without refund.
Cancellation
  • The DP and full payment are non-refundable and cannot be transferred to another person or another type of makeup. Cancellation initiated by the client is not refundable.
  • If a disaster or riot forces the cancellation of the event, payments are not refunded but can be kept as a deposit for a future booking, valid within the same year only.
  • If the artist is unable to perform the agreed work, a suitable replacement team will be provided.
Accommodation & Transport
  • Makeup at our studio: free transport.
  • Home service within Jabodetabek: transport is charged based on the round trip distance from the studio to your location, calculated with the Bluebird app.
  • Outside Jabodetabek: the pricelist excludes transport, accommodation, and hotel. Transport is charged at the round trip Bluebird fare or flight ticket price, with lodging at least equal to the bride's.
  • Outside Java: flights with Garuda, Citilink, or Batik and airport transfers to the venue are required. Makeup is charged at 2x the pricelist.
Retouch
  • Please do not remove your makeup (except on the lips) or wash or wet your hair before the retouch is complete.
  • If this is not followed, the client pays the full prevailing rate for the service instead of the retouch rate.
Booking Accuracy
  • Please book the package listed in the pricelist that matches your actual event.
  • We reserve the right to decline service and leave the location if a booking is found to have been made knowingly for a purpose that does not match its actual use.
Attire Visit & Fitting
  • A visit to view the attire can be scheduled with a DP of IDR 100.000.
  • A fitting schedule is given after the 50% DP is paid. Our admin sets the fitting date, and Wednesdays or Thursdays between 9 am and 5 pm are recommended.
  • Please arrive according to your schedule. Fitting is limited to the couple, 2 mothers, and 2 fathers.
Attire Terms
  • All wedding packages include modern accessories, a hijab veil, pashmina, and manset for the bride.
  • Attire is ready stock in our gallery, with no extra charge for plus-size attire from the gallery. A charge applies only if the size is unavailable and a new custom model is requested.
  • Groom's attire: three outfits, consisting of 2 beskap and 1 suit. The groom's shirt and loafers for the reception suit are provided by the client.
  • Bride's slippers: one neutral pair (gold or silver) is loaned. White akad slippers are loaned for the groom.
  • Mother's attire: kebaya, pleated skirt or kain, manset, hijab, veil, and angkin if needed. Father's attire: black beskap, knee-length kain with the same motif as the mother's, and peci.
  • Slippers and longtorso for mothers and guest receivers are not provided.
  • Attire can be collected after full payment, or sent via Gosend at the client's expense.
  • The deposit is refunded when the attire is returned to the gallery intact, not lost or damaged.
Preparation Tips
  • Eyelash extensions are not recommended. If you wear them, false lashes will not be applied.
  • Skip skincare on the day, including sunscreen, since skin preparation is part of the service.
  • Stop doctor-prescribed creams at least one week before, as they can make the skin slippery.
  • Keep up a daily or basic skincare routine so your skin is at its best, and use sheet masks occasionally for healthy hydration.
  • If you are booking a hairdo, wash your hair the day before so it is dry and not limp on the day.
  • Trim or thread your eyebrows for a neat finish.
  • Prepare a dedicated makeup space with a table, a chair, and AC or a fan. A table at dining-table height is most comfortable.
  • Wear a front or back opening outfit with a wide enough opening, so makeup and hairdo are not disturbed.
  • If you sweat easily or the event is outdoors, bring a portable fan to help your makeup last.
